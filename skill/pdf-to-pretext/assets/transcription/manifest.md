# Manifest

The survey of the whole document (pass 1).  Everything here is fixed before any section
is written, so that identifiers, numbering, macros, and the bibliography agree across the
sections.  Count the tables against the assembled source at the end.

## 1. Front matter

Title; authors with affiliations and emails, and where they are printed; date, keywords,
subject codes (only what the document prints); abstract; acknowledgements; funding.
